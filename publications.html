Conference AI Powered Depression Monitoring System for Sinhala and Tamil Users
Author - Sashini Sithara, Co-Author - Pasindu Sandeep, Jayani Srimali, Amasha Wijesinghe
IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing · Vol. 32, 2024

This paper proposes a novel hybrid architecture combining convolutional and recurrent neural networks for detecting AI-synthesized audio in real-time telephony environments. Our model achieves 97.3% accuracy on the ASVspoof2021 benchmark with a latency of under 80ms per inference, making it suitable for live call screening. We further introduce a novel multi-scale spectrogram fusion technique that captures both micro and macro temporal patterns in audio signals.
